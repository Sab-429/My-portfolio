About

I'm a Software Engineer focused on building reliable, scalable, and efficient backend systems. My interests lie in backend architecture, distributed systems, system design, and infrastructure engineering. I enjoy working beyond application-level development — understanding how services communicate, how data flows across distributed environments, and how systems are designed to handle concurrency, scalability, and reliability. My engineering approach revolves around writing maintainable code, understanding system-level trade-offs, and building solutions that address real-world engineering challenges. I actively explore microservices, event-driven architectures, cloud infrastructure, and automation to deepen my understanding of production-oriented systems. I believe good engineering is not just about making software work, but designing systems that remain reliable, adaptable, and maintainable as they grow.
